What Drives Us

A church built
on three purposes

Everything we do flows from our love for Christ and our desire to see his glory made known — in this congregation, and throughout our community.

01
✝

Reflecting Christ

To reflect Christ means because of God's love to us, that QPC will aim to be a loving community that welcomes, accepts and engages with all types of people from all walks of life as Jesus did.

02
🕊

Refreshing Believers

To refresh believers means because the Holy Spirit has given gifts to his people, that the leadership will provide QPC with a variety of opportunities to grow and serve.

03
🤝

Reaching Friends

To reach friends means because of Christ's love to us, that believers will develop genuine loving relationships with neighbours, friends and families so that they can share the Gospel in words and works.

Reformed & Presbyterian

What We
Believe

Scripture Alone Grace Alone Faith Alone Christ Alone To God's Glory Westminster Confession Presbyterian Order

We are a congregation of the Presbyterian Church of Australia, holding to the historic Reformed faith expressed in the Westminster Confession of Faith. We believe the Bible to be the inspired and infallible Word of God — sufficient for all of life and godliness.

Our theology is not an abstract system. It shapes how we read Scripture, how we worship, how we raise our families, and how we engage with our neighbours. We hold these truths with conviction and with humility — knowing that the God we serve is greater than any of our formulations.

If you have questions about what we believe, we'd love to talk with you. No question is too basic, and no doubt is too big.
