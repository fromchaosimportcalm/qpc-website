About Midweek Prayer Meeting

Gathering to pray for one another

The Midweek Prayer Meeting is one of the most important things we do as a congregation. Every Wednesday evening at 7:30pm, we meet via Google Meet to read Scripture, share prayer points, and pray together as a church family.

It's a relaxed and welcoming time. You don't need to be confident praying out loud — many people simply listen and agree in prayer, and that matters just as much. Whether you're at home, at work, or joining from the road, this is a meaningful way to stay connected and support one another.

If you'd like the link or need help joining the meeting for the first time, just get in touch — we'd be very glad to have you with us.
