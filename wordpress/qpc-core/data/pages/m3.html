About M3

Dinner, Discussion,
and the Workshop

M3 — the Men's Motor Ministry — began with three mates and a shared dream: to build something, eat something, and talk about something that matters. Every Thursday night at 6pm, men gather at the church manse for exactly that.

The evening opens with dinner and a discussion — Scripture, life, work, faith in the day-to-day. Then it's into the workshop, where the current project is waiting. M3's signature build is the electric eskie racer — a church-built, community-enjoyed vehicle that's become something of a local legend.

"Nothing beats the buzz of electric innovation after a hard day's work on a Thursday."

What makes M3 work isn't the project — it's the people. Men who wouldn't necessarily describe themselves as church types find that working alongside each other with their hands creates space for real conversation. Faith, doubt, family, pressure, purpose — all of it comes out in the workshop.

Whether you're a seasoned mechanic, a complete beginner, or just someone who wants a decent feed and good company on a Thursday night — you're welcome to join the circuit.
