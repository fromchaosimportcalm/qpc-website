About Bible Study Groups

Opening the Bible together

Small groups are a key part of life at QPC. They're a place to build meaningful relationships, open the Bible together, pray for one another, and encourage each other as we follow Jesus in everyday life.

We currently have two groups meeting during the week:

Tuesday Night Small Group (Googong) — This group meets on Tuesday evenings in Googong and is hosted by the Bentley family. It's a relaxed and welcoming space to read the Bible together, ask questions, share life, and pray.

Ladies Bible Study (Thursday Morning) — Our ladies Bible study meets on Thursdays at 10:30am. This group provides a supportive environment for women to study God's Word together, grow in faith, and enjoy fellowship.

If you're new to church, exploring Christianity, or looking to deepen your walk with Christ, small groups are a great place to connect.
