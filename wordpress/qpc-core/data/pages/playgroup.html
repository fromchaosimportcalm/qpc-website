About Joey's Play Group

A place to play, connect, and belong

Joey's Play Group is one of the warmest corners of QPC's weekly life. Every Monday morning, parents and carers bring their little ones to the Church Hall for a couple of hours of play, friendship, and good conversation.

It's a space where children can simply be children — playing freely while their parents get to know each other, share a coffee, and build the kind of friendships that matter. Whether you're new to the area, new to church, or just looking for a welcoming midweek gathering for your family, you're very welcome here.

No faith background required. Come as you are, bring your kids, and stay for as long as you'd like.

A simple morning that ends up meaning quite a lot.
