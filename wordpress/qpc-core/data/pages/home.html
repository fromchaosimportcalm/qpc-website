What Drives Us

A church built
on three purposes

We seek to let our love for Christ shape all we do, as we long to see his glory made known in this congregation and throughout our community.

01

✝

Reflecting Christ

Because of God's love to us, QPC will aim to be a loving community that welcomes, accepts and engages with all types of people from all walks of life as Jesus did.

02

🕊

Refreshing Believers

Because the Holy Spirit has given gifts to his people, the leadership will provide QPC with a variety of opportunities to grow and serve.

03

🤝

Reaching Friends

Because of Christ's love to us, believers will develop genuine loving relationships with neighbours, friends and families so that they can share the Gospel in words and works.

Reformed & Presbyterian

What We
Believe

Scripture Alone Grace Alone Faith Alone Christ Alone To God's Glory Westminster Confession Presbyterian Order

We are a congregation of the Presbyterian Church of Australia, holding to the historic Reformed faith expressed in the Westminster Confession of Faith. We believe the Bible to be the inspired and infallible Word of God - sufficient for all of life and godliness.

Our theology is not an abstract system. It shapes how we read Scripture, how we worship, how we raise our families, and how we engage with our neighbours. We hold these truths with conviction and with humility.

If you have questions about what we believe, we'd love to talk with you. No question is too basic, and no doubt is too big.
