About the Gala

Bring someone to the table

The Christmas Gala is a chance to sit down to a proper Christmas meal: fully catered, served on real plates, with good company all around. Just a warm, relaxed evening together at the Southern Pavilion.

It's also the perfect night to invite your friends, coworkers, neighbours and family, especially those who wouldn't normally come to church. There's no catch and nothing expected of anyone. It's simply a chance to share Christmas together and get to know the people of QPC.

Tickets are $30 per adult. Children are $15 if they need a meal, and free if they don't.
