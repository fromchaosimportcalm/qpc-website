About Men's Brekkie

More than the first meal of the day

Men's Brekkie is a weekly Wednesday morning ritual that starts the day well. We gather at 8am in the Church Hall for a hearty breakfast, real conversation, and a short devotional that gives the day some direction before work begins.

It's intentionally simple. No programme, no pressure. Men from all walks of life sit down together — tradesmen, teachers, retired blokes, young dads — and share what's actually going on in their lives. The devotional is short but it lands, and the conversation that follows is usually the best part.

Whether you're a long-time churchgoer or just curious, you're welcome at the table. The eggs are on us — hopefully not burnt.

The kind of morning you wish every Wednesday started like.
